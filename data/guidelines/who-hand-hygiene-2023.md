## Scope
This guideline applies to all health workers who provide direct patient care in hospitals, clinics and long-term care facilities. It replaces the 2019 edition.

## Recommendations
- Perform hand hygiene at five moments: before touching a patient, before a clean or aseptic procedure, after body fluid exposure risk, after touching a patient, and after touching patient surroundings.
- Use an alcohol-based hand rub for 20 to 30 seconds when hands are not visibly soiled.
- Wash with soap and water for 40 to 60 seconds when hands are visibly soiled or contaminated with blood or body fluids.
- Artificial nails and nail extensions are prohibited for staff in clinical areas. Keep natural nails short.

## Implementation
Place hand rub dispensers at the point of care. Train all clinical staff at induction and annually. Audit compliance by direct observation every quarter and aim for at least 80 percent compliance on each ward.

## Changes from the 2019 edition
The recommended duration for hand rub increased from 15 to 20 seconds to 20 to 30 seconds. The duration for washing with soap and water increased from 30 seconds to 40 to 60 seconds. Artificial nails moved from discouraged to prohibited. Compliance audits moved from twice a year to quarterly.
