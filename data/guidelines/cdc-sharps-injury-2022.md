## Scope
This guidance covers prevention of needlestick and sharps injuries and the response after an exposure.

## Recommendations
- Use safety-engineered needles and devices whenever they are available.
- Do not recap used needles. If recapping is unavoidable, use a one-handed scoop technique.
- Place sharps in a puncture-resistant container at the point of use and replace the container when it is three quarters full.
- After an injury, wash the wound with soap and water, report the incident immediately to your supervisor and occupational health.
- Begin post-exposure assessment as soon as possible, ideally within 2 hours, and arrange baseline blood testing for the exposed worker and, with consent, the source patient.

## Implementation
Review all sharps injuries quarterly to find trends. Provide training on safe use of devices to every new employee and when a new device is introduced.
