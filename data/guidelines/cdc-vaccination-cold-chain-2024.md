## Scope
This guidance covers storage, monitoring and transport of vaccines in clinics, pharmacies and hospitals.

## Recommendations
- Store refrigerated vaccines between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius. Store frozen vaccines between minus 50 and minus 15 degrees Celsius.
- Use a digital data logger with a buffered probe in every storage unit and read the temperature at the start and the end of each clinic day.
- Never store vaccines in the door of the unit or in produce drawers. Keep them in the centre, away from the walls and the cooling element.
- After a temperature excursion, move the affected vaccines to a working unit, label them as do not use, and contact the manufacturer for guidance before any are administered.
- Use qualified transport containers for off-site clinics and record the temperature during transport.

## Implementation
Name a primary and a backup vaccine coordinator. Connect storage units to backup power or have a written emergency plan. Keep temperature logs for at least three years.
