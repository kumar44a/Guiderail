## Scope
This document describes how clinics should detect, report and follow up adverse events after immunisation.

## Recommendations
- Report any serious adverse event, such as anaphylaxis, hospitalisation or death, within 24 hours of becoming aware of it.
- Report non-serious events within 7 days through the national adverse event reporting system.
- Record the vaccine product, lot number, date, site of administration and onset time for every report.
- Assess causality using a standard checklist and classify each event as consistent, indeterminate, inconsistent or unclassifiable.
- Follow up serious events for at least 30 days or until they resolve.

## Implementation
Name an adverse event focal point in each clinic. Review all reports at a monthly safety meeting and send summaries to the immunisation programme manager.
