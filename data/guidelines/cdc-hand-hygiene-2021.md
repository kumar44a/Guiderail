## Scope
This guidance covers healthcare personnel in acute care, ambulatory and long-term care settings.

## Recommendations
- Alcohol-based hand rub is preferred in most clinical situations. Rub until hands are dry, which usually takes about 20 seconds.
- Use soap and water when hands are visibly dirty, and after caring for a patient with Clostridioides difficile or norovirus, because alcohol is less effective against spores and non-enveloped viruses.
- Clean hands before donning gloves and again after removing gloves.
- Do not wear artificial fingernails when in direct contact with patients at high risk, such as those in intensive care or the operating room.

## Implementation
Make hand rub available at every patient room entrance. Monitor adherence and give feedback to units monthly.
