## Scope
This guidance applies to healthcare personnel who develop symptoms of an acute respiratory infection.

## Recommendations
- Staff with symptoms must stay away from work and must not provide patient care.
- Staff may return to work 10 days after symptom onset, provided they have been fever-free for at least 24 hours without fever-reducing medication and symptoms have improved.
- Staff who work with severely immunocompromised patients should be reassigned until they have completed the 10-day period.

## Implementation
Staff must report symptoms to occupational health before the start of their next shift. Occupational health keeps a record of absence dates and return dates.
