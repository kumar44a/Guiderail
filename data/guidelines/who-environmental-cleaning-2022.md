## Scope
This guideline covers routine and outbreak cleaning of patient rooms, wards and shared areas in health facilities.

## Recommendations
- High-touch surfaces include bed rails, door handles, call buttons, light switches, tray tables and toilet flush handles. Clean them at least twice a day.
- Clean low-touch surfaces such as walls and floors at least once a day and whenever they are visibly soiled.
- Clean from the cleanest area to the dirtiest area and from high surfaces to low surfaces.
- Clean with detergent before applying a disinfectant. Respect the manufacturer contact time.
- Use 0.1 percent sodium hypochlorite for routine disinfection and 0.5 percent for blood and body fluid spills.

## Implementation
Use single-use cloths or launder them daily at high temperature. Change cleaning solutions at the start of each shift and after cleaning an isolation room. Keep a cleaning checklist in every room.
