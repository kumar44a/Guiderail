## Scope
This guidance applies to healthcare personnel who may be exposed to infectious agents while delivering care.

## Recommendations
- Choose PPE according to the anticipated exposure and the type of transmission precautions in place.
- Use an N95 or equivalent respirator for airborne precautions. Medical masks are sufficient for routine droplet precautions.
- Wear eye protection or a face shield when splashes or sprays are anticipated.
- Remove gloves first, then perform hand hygiene, then remove other PPE. The gown and respirator come off last.
- Do not wear the same gown between patients, even for extended use, unless the patients are cohorted for the same infection.

## Implementation
Annual respirator fit testing is required for staff who use tight-fitting respirators. Keep a log of fit-test results and retest after any facial change that could affect the seal.
