## Scope
This guideline sets out how health workers should select, put on, take off and dispose of personal protective equipment (PPE).

## Recommendations
- Select PPE based on a risk assessment of the task, the patient and the likely exposure to blood, body fluids or respiratory droplets.
- Use gloves, a gown and eye protection when splashes or contact with body fluids are likely. Add a medical mask for droplet precautions and a particulate respirator for airborne precautions.
- Put on PPE in this order: gown, mask or respirator, eye protection, gloves.
- Take off PPE in this order: gloves, eye protection, gown, mask or respirator. Perform hand hygiene immediately after removal.
- Single-use gloves must never be reused or washed.

## Implementation
Fit-test every respirator user at first use and then once a year. Provide PPE at the point of use and display donning and doffing posters. Observe a sample of staff donning and doffing each quarter.
