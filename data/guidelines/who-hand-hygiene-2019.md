## Scope
This guideline applies to all health workers who provide direct patient care in hospitals, clinics and long-term care facilities.

## Recommendations
- Perform hand hygiene at five moments: before touching a patient, before a clean or aseptic procedure, after body fluid exposure risk, after touching a patient, and after touching patient surroundings.
- Use an alcohol-based hand rub for 15 to 20 seconds when hands are not visibly soiled.
- Wash with soap and water for 30 seconds when hands are visibly soiled or contaminated with blood or body fluids.
- Rings and artificial nails are discouraged in clinical areas.

## Implementation
Place hand rub dispensers at the point of care. Train all clinical staff at induction and annually. Audit compliance by direct observation twice a year.
