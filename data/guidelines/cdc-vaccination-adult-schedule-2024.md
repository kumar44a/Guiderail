## Scope
This guidance sets out vaccines recommended for healthcare personnel and the practice steps for giving them.

## Recommendations
- Give influenza vaccine every year to all healthcare personnel.
- Give one dose of Tdap, then a Td or Tdap booster every 10 years.
- Vaccinate healthcare personnel against hepatitis B and check anti-HBs levels 1 to 2 months after the final dose of the series.
- Confirm documented immunity to measles, mumps and rubella and to varicella, and give vaccine to staff without evidence of immunity.
- Screen every recipient for contraindications and precautions before vaccination.
- Observe recipients for 15 minutes after vaccination, and for 30 minutes if they have a history of severe allergic reaction.

## Implementation
Record each dose in the immunisation registry and in the employee health record. Keep epinephrine and trained staff available wherever vaccines are given.
