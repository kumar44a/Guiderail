## Scope
This guideline covers segregation, storage and transport of waste generated by health care activities.

## Recommendations
- Segregate waste at the point of generation into colour-coded containers: yellow for infectious waste, red for sharps in puncture-proof containers, and black for general waste.
- Do not mix infectious waste with general waste.
- Store waste in a secure, ventilated area for no longer than 48 hours in temperate climates and 24 hours in hot climates.
- Handlers must wear gloves, a gown or apron, boots and eye protection, and must be vaccinated against hepatitis B.

## Implementation
Use dedicated trolleys for waste transport and clean them daily. Keep a waste register and review volumes each month.
