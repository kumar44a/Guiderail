## Scope
This document describes how NIH-sponsored panels rate the body of evidence behind a clinical recommendation.

## Recommendations
- Rate the strength of evidence as high, moderate, low or insufficient.
- Use the rating insufficient when the evidence cannot be assessed because studies are missing, too small or contradictory.
- Assess each outcome separately and consider study design, directness, consistency and precision.
- Panels should record a recommendation as supported only when strength of evidence is at least moderate. Otherwise record it as a consensus statement.

## Notes
Consensus statements must be labelled as expert opinion and must not be presented as evidence-based recommendations.
