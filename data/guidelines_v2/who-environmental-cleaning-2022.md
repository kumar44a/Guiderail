## Scope
This guideline covers routine and outbreak cleaning of patient rooms, wards and shared areas in health facilities.

## Recommendations
- High-touch surfaces include bed rails, door handles, call buttons, light switches, tray tables and toilet flush handles. Clean them at least twice a day.
- Clean low-touch surfaces such as walls and floors at least once a day and whenever they are visibly soiled.
- Clean from the cleanest area to the dirtiest area and from high surfaces to low surfaces.
- Clean with detergent before applying a disinfectant. Respect the manufacturer contact time.
- Use 0.1 percent sodium hypochlorite for routine disinfection and 0.5 percent for blood and body fluid spills.

## Implementation
Use single-use cloths or launder them daily at high temperature. Change cleaning solutions at the start of each shift and after cleaning an isolation room. Keep a cleaning checklist in every room.

## Cleaning equipment
Use colour-coded cloths and mops: red for toilets, blue for general areas, green for kitchens and yellow for isolation rooms. Wash mops at 60 degrees Celsius and dry them fully before reuse. Never leave mops standing in dirty water. Replace worn equipment every three months.

## Terminal cleaning
After a patient with an infection is discharged, remove all linen and waste, then clean from top to bottom. Wipe the mattress and bed frame, the bedside table, the sink and the bathroom. Allow surfaces to dry before the bed is made. Record the time and the name of the cleaner.

## Spills
Cover a blood spill with absorbent material, then pour 0.5 percent sodium hypochlorite over it and leave it for 10 minutes. Wearing gloves and an apron, remove the material into a yellow bag. Clean the area with detergent and water and dry it.

## Training and supervision
New cleaners are trained for two days before working alone. Supervisors inspect at least five rooms per shift using a checklist and an ultraviolet marker or an adenosine triphosphate meter. Rooms scoring under 80 percent are re-cleaned and the cleaner is retrained.
