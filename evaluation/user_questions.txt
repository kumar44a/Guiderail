#   1. Open the PDFs in data/real/ (isolation guideline, vaccine storage toolkit, toolkit resources list,
#      WHO hand hygiene summary, WHO 2026 newsletter).
#   2. Pick a fact. Ask about it the way a nurse, coordinator or manager would actually ask, in your own words.
#      Don't copy the document's wording. Typos, short fragments and informal phrasing are all welcome.
#   3. Mix the documents (aim for a few from each) and mix question types: how long / how many / what / when /
#      where / yes-no / "what should I do if...".
#   4. Include about 5 questions the documents do NOT answer (write NONE as the expected answer).
#   5. Do not look at how the system answers a question before writing it down here. Questions you have already tried
#      in the app are fine to list, but mark them with the word TRIED at the end of the line.
