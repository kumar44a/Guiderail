Second sealed run. Written and committed BEFORE these 50 questions were run on the system.

Set: real corpus, split "sealed2" = V01-V50, the owner's second batch ("Next Set of 50 questions"), pasted without expected answers.
The assistant labelled them from the PDF text (data/real, hand hygiene summary 2009) before any run. 48 are labelled answerable, 2 are labelled
not in the documents (V43 gloves on damp hands; V45 gloves and a false sense of security). check_labels.py confirms every expected fact exists in the
relevant document (0 problems). Nothing was tried on the system first.

Known weaknesses of this set, stated in advance:
- The questions are long, formal and often compound ("what is X and which Y"), unlike the short natural wording of the first set. They look machine-written
  and were probably drawn from the full 2009 guideline, of which the corpus holds only the summary. Several ask for a reason ("why...") where the
  document states only the rule. Those are labelled weak in the record note and score on the rule.
- Roughly a third of the expected facts are deliberately loose (one word such as "feedback" or "reduc"), because no single phrase answers the question.
  "Answer correct" therefore over-credits; validated precision on the weak-labelled questions must be read with that in mind.
- Only 2 of 50 are out of scope, so the out-of-scope false-accept rate rests on n=2 and says almost nothing.

Configuration: frozen (same as the first sealed run: hybrid retrieval, validator rounds 2 and 3, 80-word chunks, rewriting off).
Run once with the extractive generator and once with the Claude generator. Both runs are logged in test_split_runs.log.
Nothing is changed afterwards on the strength of these results. Reported as found, with the first sealed set kept separate (they are not pooled).

CORRECTION found after the run (not before): the rules above name V43 and V45 as the two out-of-scope questions. The ids are wrong; they are V42 and V44.
Worse, V42 (gloves on damp hands) was labelled out of scope by mistake. The summary says "donning gloves while hands are still wet from either washing or
applying alcohol increases the risk of skin irritation". The assistant's keyword search missed it (it searched "damp"); the system found it. The label is
corrected to in-corpus. The saved run reports keep the ORIGINAL label (V42 counted as out of scope); the generated report applies the corrected one, and the
effect is stated there. The corrected set has 49 answerable questions and 1 out of scope (V44), so the out-of-scope check rests on a single question.
This is one more reason to read the answerable-question results with caution: the labeller (the assistant) is fallible in both directions.
