Retrieval round 6: chunk size. Written and committed BEFORE any code or measurement.

Why: round 5's diagnosis. Facts inside long chunks of mostly other text (a figure, a list) are diluted for both the hashed vectors and BM25.
The chunk budget (80 words) has never been varied. Shorter chunks should let a short fact stand on its own; the cost is less context per chunk.

Candidates: maximum chunk length of 50 words and of 35 words (the baseline is the kept state of round 3: 80 words, hybrid on, rewriting off).
Everything else unchanged. The index is rebuilt for each candidate; documents, loader and retrieval code are untouched. The neighbour fallback for
very short chunks (fewer than 25 words) is unchanged.

Judged exactly as in round 3: real-dev Hit@5 must rise by at least one query or MRR by at least 0.02 with no fall in Hit@5, and the guards hold:
G1 synthetic dev Hit@5 not lower and MRR not lower by more than 0.01; G2 earlier real dev queries' Hit@5 not lower; G3 with the extractive generator,
dev wrong-but-validated answers and out-of-scope accepts do not rise on either corpus; G4 validator stress not worse; G5 index builds byte-identical.
If both candidates pass, the one with the higher real-dev MRR is kept. If neither passes, 80 words stays.
Because chunk size changes how much context the generator and validator see, a candidate that passes must also be checked end to end with the Claude
generator on dev (once): out-of-scope accepts must not rise and wrong-but-validated answers must not rise by more than 1.
Then: freeze, regenerate the report tables on the shipped configuration, run the sealed set once per generator. Nothing is tuned on it.

ROUND 6 OUTCOME (written after the measurements)
  50 words: real-dev Hit@5 41 -> 40, MRR 0.6566 -> 0.6655; synthetic MRR 0.825 -> 0.804; stress set worse; extractive out-of-scope accepts up. REVERTED.
  35 words: real-dev Hit@5 41 -> 37, MRR 0.6566 -> 0.6027. REVERTED.
  80 words stays. (judge outputs: retrieval_round3_r6W50.json, retrieval_round3_r6W35.json)
Net result of rounds 3-6 on retrieval: only hybrid BM25 + FAISS fusion (B) was kept; Claude query rewriting (round 5) is available but off by default.
