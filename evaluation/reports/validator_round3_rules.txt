Validator round 3. Written and committed BEFORE any code change and before any measurement.

Why: end to end, the Claude generator answers about four in five real-dev questions correctly, but the validator withholds about one in five of the
correct answers (9 of 50 in the latest dev run; reasons read from the validator's own recorded components, no new run):
  - low question support although the answer is right: the answer says "refrigerator" or "stabilize", the question said "fridge" or "settle" (6 cases);
  - number checks that misfire: "1:10" read as unsupported because the PDF glues a footnote onto it ("1:10.303"), and "2026 posters" read as an
    unsupported quantity because the word after a number is treated as its unit (3 cases).
Scope: the validator only. Retrieval, generators, prompts, threshold (0.6), weights and floors are untouched. Fixes are judged one at a time, cumulatively,
in this order:
  V1  The number-with-unit check applies only when the word after the number is a real unit: second, minute, hour, day, week, month, year, feet, foot,
      inch, cm, mm, meter, metre, degree, C, F, percent, %, mg, ml, mcg, g, kg, dose. The plain check that every number in the answer occurs in the
      evidence is unchanged.
  V2  An evidence number written as digits, a point and exactly three digits ("10.303") also supports its integer part ("10"). This repairs numbers that the
      PDF text extraction fused with a footnote reference. It cannot support a decimal such as "10.5".
  V3  Question support also credits question terms that occur in the text of the CITED passages, not only in the answer and the cited titles. If a cited
      passage contains the question's words, the passage is about the question even when the answer does not repeat them.
Material: only reports made with the CURRENT retrieval configuration, so the evidence can be rebuilt exactly: real and synthetic dev, extractive
(retrieval round 3's kept state, tag r3B) and Claude (latest runs, tag r5Lbase), plus the validator stress set. evaluation/revalidate.py must first reproduce
every stored confidence exactly under the current validator (--verify); if it does not, stop. No API calls are made in this round until the final run.
Keep rule for each fix, on that material (all must hold):
  A  in no report does the number of wrong-but-validated answers or of out-of-scope accepts go up;
  B  no stress case decided correctly before is decided wrongly after, and the share of bad answers sent to review does not fall;
  C  at least one correct answer that was withheld is validated.
If A fails for V3 the fix is reverted whole; no weaker variant is tried in this round. Later fixes are judged without a reverted one.
After the last kept fix: freeze, regenerate the report tables on the shipped configuration, run the sealed set once per generator. Nothing is tuned on it.
Expectation stated in advance: V1 and V2 are narrow and should pass. V3 loosens a check and may well fail A on the extractive reports, which is the
reason it is judged on them.

OUTCOME (written after the judgement; the rules above were committed first)
All three fixes met their rules on the saved dev answers (evaluation/reports/validator_round3.json): V1 released two correct answers (one extractive, one Claude),
V2 released two (Claude), V3 released one (synthetic Claude); none created a wrong-but-validated answer or an out-of-scope accept in any of the four reports;
the stress set was unchanged. V3 did not release the low-question-support answers that motivated it (their question words, such as "fridge" and "settle", are not
in the cited text either), so its benefit is small and its risk is not zero. All three are shipped as settings in validator.py.
